(agent-main)>>>**Message tree** — a hierarchical (parent/child) arrangement of messages, where each message is attached to the one it responds to or derives from.

- **Definition & shape:** The original message sits at the root; each reply or follow-up becomes a child node, so a conversation fans out into branches instead of a flat list. Every node typically carries the message itself plus links to its parent and children.
- **How it's built:** The links come either from explicit metadata — e.g., email's `Message-ID` / `In-Reply-To` / `References` headers, or a thread ID in chat apps — or are inferred from sender, subject, and timestamps when no such fields exist.
- **Why it's used:** It powers threaded conversations in email, forums, and chat (letting clients show, collapse, or expand branches), and the same idea appears in distributed systems and blockchains, where a tree/DAG of messages tracks causal order and helps detect forks, duplicates, or out-of-order delivery.

Note: "message tree" means slightly different things across fields (email threading, messaging middleware, distributed protocols); the common thread is the parent-child reply structure.
